# Pinterest Plan (about 1 hr/day, free)

Goal: 5-10 pins a day linking to Etsy listings. DRAFT, nothing posted.

**Daily routine**
1. Pick 1-2 products. Create 3-5 pin variants each (different mockup image, different title).
2. Schedule them in Pinterest's scheduler, spaced across the day.
3. Link to the Etsy listing, or to the site for the freebie.

**Pin title formulas**
- "[Cute thing] [Printable/Sticker] | Instant Digital Download"
- "Fall Planner Stickers You Can Print at Home"
- "Gift Tag Printables for Christmas (Snowman)"

**Boards:** Fall & Halloween Printables, Christmas Printables, Nursery Wall Art, Planner & Journal Stickers.

**Sunday review:** note the 3 pins with the most saves/clicks, make more like them, retire the bottom 3.
